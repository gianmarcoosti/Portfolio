Video Games Sales Trend Analysis

This project analyzes video game sales trends over the years, focusing on platform performance, regional sales, and genre popularity.
The dataset includes historical sales data for video games across multiple platforms.

View on Kaggle

Project Overview

  • Dataset: Video Game Sales.
  • Tools Used: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn.
  • Key Metrics: Global Sales, Regional Sales (NA, EU, JP, Other), Platform Performance, Genre Popularity.
  • Visualizations: Bar charts, Pie charts, Line graphs, Heatmaps.

Key Insights

  • Identified the top-selling platforms and genres globally.
  • Analyzed regional sales trends (North America, Europe, Japan, Others).
  • Visualized the correlation between critic scores and sales.
  • Explored the decline of certain platforms over time.

Global Video Game Sales: Four Decades of Growth

Explore the journey from arcade classics to modern gaming (1980-2020).

The 8-bit Era (1980s)

The birth of home console gaming with Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) dominating the market after the video game crash of 1983.

Avg. Annual Sales: $35M

The Console Wars (1990s)

Fierce competition between Sega and Nintendo, followed by Sony's entrance with PlayStation revolutionizing the industry with 3D graphics.

Avg. Annual Sales: $172M

Mainstream Explosion (2000s)

Gaming becomes a mainstream entertainment medium with Xbox, PlayStation 2, and Nintendo Wii bringing new audiences to gaming.

Avg. Annual Sales: $505M

Digital Revolution (2010s)

Rise of digital distribution, mobile gaming, and games as a service models transform the industry's business landscape.

Avg. Annual Sales: $431M

Key Gaming Milestones

1983

Video game market crash in North America, known as the "Atari Shock." The industry lost 97% of revenue in two years.

1985

Nintendo releases the NES in North America, revitalizing the gaming industry with titles like Super Mario Bros.

1994

Sony enters the market with PlayStation, bringing CD-ROM technology and 3D graphics to console gaming.

2001

Microsoft enters the console market with the original Xbox, beginning the three-way console competition.

2006

Nintendo launches the Wii with motion controls, appealing to casual gamers and achieving record-breaking sales.

2007

Global video game sales peak at $677 million, marking the height of the industry's growth in this period.

2010

Mobile gaming rises with smartphones, introducing new business models and expanding the global player base.

2017

Nintendo Switch launches, blending home console and portable gaming in one device, revitalizing Nintendo's position.
