Nova Architecture

Redefining urban spaces through sustainable design and innovative materials.

Details

  • Client Nova Architecture Group
  • Year 2023
  • Location Berlin, Germany

Description

Nova Architecture approached us to create a digital presence that reflects their innovative approach to sustainable urban design. The challenge was to translate their physical architectural philosophy into a digital experience that maintains their signature aesthetic of clean lines, natural materials, and environmental consciousness.

Our solution was a modular design system that mirrors their architectural process, with interactive 3D models of their projects, detailed material studies, and a content strategy focused on their sustainability initiatives. The result is a digital platform that serves as both a portfolio and an educational resource for sustainable design.

Brand Identity Web Design Art Direction 3D Visualization

Process

Our collaboration began with immersive workshops at Nova's Berlin studio, studying their design methodology and material research. We developed a digital language that echoes their physical design principles.

Challenges

Translating complex architectural concepts into intuitive digital experiences while maintaining load-time performance for high-resolution 3D models and material studies.

Results

A 40% increase in client inquiries, recognition in architectural publications for digital innovation, and adoption of our platform as a teaching tool in design schools.
